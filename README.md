This is a code that can be used in vscode as soundtests or languange tests, its free for everyone to use! 
It currently has 7 different languanges and those are as followed : 
German , french , Turkish , English , Spanish , Italic and Russian
Libraries includes : sounddevice , numpy , scipy.io.wavfile , speech_recognition , googletrans
Its free for everyone to use feel free to develop your own if you would like to!
